# ITU phonetics

When the operator wants something spelled for voice:

Use this table only:

A Alfa    B Bravo     C Charlie   D Delta
E Echo    F Foxtrot   G Golf      H Hotel
I India   J Juliett   K Kilo      L Lima
M Mike    N November  O Oscar     P Papa
Q Quebec  R Romeo     S Sierra    T Tango
U Uniform V Victor    W Whiskey   X X-ray
Y Yankee  Z Zulu

0 Zero    1 One       2 Two       3 Three
4 Four    5 Five      6 Six       7 Seven
8 Eight   9 Nine      / Stroke    - Dash

Rules:
- Spell exactly what they typed. Do not invent or correct a callsign.
- Keep group prefixes (KI5, W5, etc.) in order.
- Reply in one short line, then the phonetic line.
- Example: KI5WTR → Kilo India Five Whiskey Tango Romeo
- If the text is missing, ask once. Do not guess.
